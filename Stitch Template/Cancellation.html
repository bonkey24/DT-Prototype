Cancellation & Refund Rules (Indian Railways)

verified

Standard Indian Railways & IRCTC Refund Protection

Refunds are credited directly back to your original source account (UPI / Net Banking / Card) automatically.

currency_rupee

More than 48 hours before departure

Flat clerkage fee deduction only (₹180 for 3A / AC Chair Car, ₹120 for Sleeper, ₹240 for 2A/1A). Remaining fare is 100% refunded to your original payment account.

schedule

Between 48 hours and 12 hours before departure

25% of total fare deducted, subject to minimum clerkage charge. Balance 75% refunded immediately.

timer_off

Between 12 hours and 4 hours before departure

50% of total fare deducted (minimum clerkage applies). Note: Strictly zero refund after train chart is prepared (approx. 4 hours before train departure).

RAC & Waitlisted (WL) Ticket Cancellation

RAC or Waitlisted tickets cancelled up to 30 minutes before scheduled departure are charged only flat clerkage (₹60 + GST per passenger). Fully waitlisted e-tickets that remain unconfirmed after chart preparation are automatically cancelled and refunded 100% in full with no clerkage deduction.

Train Delayed by More Than 3 Hours

If your train is delayed by more than 3 hours at your boarding station, file an online TDR before the actual departure of the train to obtain a full 100% refund without any cancellation deductions.

Train Cancelled by Railways

If the train is cancelled due to weather, accidents, or operational reasons, a 100% full refund is automatically initiated to your bank account. No TDR filing is required for e-tickets.

Refund Processing Timelines

Online cancellations reflect back in your bank account or UPI source within 3 to 5 working days. TDR cases approved by Zonal Railways are settled within 15 to 30 working days.

Need help canceling? call Call Railway helpline 139 or file TDR online with EasyRail.
